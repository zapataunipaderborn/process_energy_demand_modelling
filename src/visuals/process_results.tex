Description: Process discovery methods are Alpha (naive miner baseline), Best Petri Net (best discovered Petri Net), Best Petri Net + Budget (best Petri Net + duration budgeting at simulation time), each crossed with the three activity-duration predictors (baseline samples from the fitted statistical distributions, ml\_local is a ML model per activity and ml\_global is a ML model for all activities). Fitness, Precision, Generalization and Simplicity are discovery quality (higher is better); Evt-Ratio Error is the relation between total simulated events/number of real events, lower is better; and the Activity duration errors are the difference of the individual activity durations vs the observed in the test set and the Lead time errors are the same but for the total case time (lower is better). \textbf{Bold} = best per column. Activity durations are reported as WAPE rather than MAE: MAE averages absolute minute errors over the activity types a case shares with its simulation, and that set differs per method, so a model that reproduces more short activities scores a lower MAE without being more accurate. The Lead time WAPE is pooled over all test cases: the summed absolute lead-time errors divided by the summed real lead times; absolute-minute columns are not reported, since lead times differ by an order of magnitude across processes. Fitness, Precision, Generalization and Simplicity are model-level and are therefore reported as the median across processes; the Activity duration WAPE is the median over all test cases, and the Lead time WAPE is pooled over them.
